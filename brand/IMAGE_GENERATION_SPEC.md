# ERC Academy Image Generation Specification

## Objective
Generate new ERC Academy social posts without requiring the user to re-supply the full branding instructions each time.

## Required output
- Vertical Instagram-friendly poster, typically 4:5.
- ERC Academy brand styling from BRAND_GUIDE.md.
- Use approved character/body reference images when available.
- Preserve the same face, hairstyle, glasses, beard, clothing style, and body fit.
- Create a fresh pose/composition for each post.

## Prompt skeleton

Create a professional vertical ERC Academy educational poster in Spanish.

Brand system:
- deep navy / royal blue background
- electric-blue diagonal edge accents
- thin gold dividers
- white ERC ACADEMY header
- APRENDE CON CIENCIA • [NUMBER]
- oversized cyan + white uppercase headline
- concise Spanish explanatory copy
- cyan PROBALO section
- large cyan rounded CTA bar
- source footer left
- ercacademynic.com right

Character:
Use the approved recurring male reference. Preserve face and body proportions, curly dark hair, round black glasses, short beard and mustache, black long-sleeve shirt, black pants, black sneakers. Use a natural teaching pose relevant to the topic.

Lighting:
Dark studio look. Restrained exposure, brightness, contrast, saturation, HDR and glow. Do not brighten the image beyond the approved sample posts.

Content:
- Headline: [HEADLINE]
- Explanation: [EXPLANATION]
- Visual metaphor / props: [VISUAL]
- PROBALO: [EXERCISE]
- CTA question: [CTA]
- Source: [SOURCE]

Do not make the design childish. Keep mobile readability high.

## Character consistency
When reference images are supplied:
- prioritize identity preservation over stylization
- preserve body fit and proportions
- preserve glasses shape and facial hair
- avoid changing age or physique
- avoid excessive skin smoothing
- avoid dramatically different lighting

## Copy density
Poster text should be short enough to scan quickly. The caption can carry additional explanation.

## Quality check before approval
Confirm:
- numbering is correct
- title hierarchy is readable
- face/body continuity is acceptable
- no accidental brightness/contrast increase
- no text collision
- no duplicated topic
- source is present
- CTA is present

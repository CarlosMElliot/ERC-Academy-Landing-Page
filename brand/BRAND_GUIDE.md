# ERC Academy Brand Guide

## Purpose
Canonical visual and editorial reference for ERC Academy social content, especially the **Aprender con ciencia** series.

## Core visual identity
- Background: deep navy / royal-blue gradient.
- Edge treatment: electric-blue diagonal framing and geometric accents.
- Accent colors: cyan and restrained gold.
- Header: **ERC ACADEMY** in white, uppercase, generous tracking.
- Divider: thin gold rule.
- Series line: **APRENDE/APRENDER CON CIENCIA • [number]**.
- Headline: oversized, uppercase, heavy sans serif. Mix cyan and white for hierarchy.
- Body copy: concise Spanish, mobile-readable.
- Section label: cyan **PROBALO**.
- CTA: large cyan rounded rectangle near the bottom with a question and the line **Contanos en los comentarios**.
- Footer: source at bottom-left; **ercacademynic.com** at bottom-right.

## Tone
Professional, scientific, energetic, modern, educational. Avoid childish styling.

## Lighting and grading
- Dark studio mood.
- Keep brightness and contrast restrained.
- Do not boost exposure, saturation, contrast, HDR, or glow beyond the approved sample look.
- Blue rim light is acceptable when subtle.

## Recurring on-camera visual identity
Use the approved recurring male character based on the stored reference images:
- Curly dark hair
- Round black glasses
- Short beard / mustache
- Black long-sleeve shirt
- Black pants
- Black sneakers with light soles
- Natural, approachable teacher / presenter presence

Preserve face and body proportions consistently across posts.

## Pose rotation
Avoid repeating the same standing pose. Rotate:
- Front-facing
- Three-quarter view
- Profile
- Walking
- Pointing
- Presenting with open palm
- Arms crossed
- Hands in pockets
- Seated
- Listening
- Writing
- Holding notebook / clipboard / laptop
- Interacting with graphic elements

## Composition rules
- Alternate subject position left/right where practical.
- Leave clean space for headlines.
- Props and icons must explain the concept, not decorate randomly.
- Keep text blocks readable on mobile.
- Never crowd the footer or CTA.

## Spanish voice
Prefer natural Nicaraguan voseo where appropriate:
- escuchá
- probalo
- contanos
- recordá
- elegí
- explicá
- planificá
- comprobá

## Instagram
Each post must be created with a corresponding Spanish Instagram caption. The caption should expand on the idea rather than duplicate all poster text verbatim.

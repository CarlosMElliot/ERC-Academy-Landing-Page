# Aprender con ciencia — Series Specification

## Purpose
Science-informed English learning content for ERC Academy.

## Structure
Every post should include:
1. ERC Academy header
2. Series number
3. Strong headline
4. One clear educational claim
5. Short explanation
6. A practical PROBALO activity
7. A CTA question
8. Source credit
9. ercacademynic.com
10. Spanish Instagram caption

## Editorial approach
- Explain one idea per post.
- Translate research or learning science into practical English-learning behavior.
- Avoid overclaiming.
- Distinguish research evidence from interpretation.
- Keep the poster simpler than the caption.

## Topic rotation
Rotate between:
- listening
- retrieval
- memory
- pronunciation
- vocabulary
- confidence / beliefs
- CEFR abilities
- study planning
- speaking tasks
- attention
- deliberate practice
- feedback
- repetition
- multimodal learning
- habit design

Avoid repeating the same theme in back-to-back posts unless it is a deliberate mini-series.
